💡 What to paste in

  • A camera with a web stream (MJPEG or a snapshot): paste its address, like http://192.168.1.50:8080/video, and pick MJPEG Stream / Snapshot URL.
  • An old Android phone: install the IP Webcam app and tap Start server. Its stream is the address it shows, plus /video (no slash at the end).
  • A security or IP camera using RTSP (rtsp://user:pass@192.168.1.50:554/stream): browsers can't play that. Run go2rtc beside the app to turn it into MJPEG, then paste go2rtc's address.
  • Away from home: a camera's home address only works at home. go2rtc shared through Tailscale works anywhere. go2rtc.yaml, beside docker-compose.yml, has the steps.

A camera mounted sideways or upside down can be turned with Rotation above, or later with ↻ Rotate on the Webcams page.
