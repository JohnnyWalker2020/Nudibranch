Estimate only. RO/DI output changes with water pressure, water temperature, membrane age and how clogged the filters are, so the real time can be quite different. Worked out from the label it can easily be off by a third or more; a timed fill is much closer. Check on the container well before this time is up, and use a float valve or auto shut-off if you have one. Watching the fill is your job: if it overflows and your room becomes an aquarium, that's on you.
