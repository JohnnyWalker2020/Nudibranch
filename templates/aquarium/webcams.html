💡 Quick Tip: Hooking Up IP & RTSP Cameras

Most security or tank cameras broadcast RTSP (e.g. rtsp://user:pass@192.168.1.50:554/stream). Browsers cannot play RTSP directly. The included docker-compose.yml has an optional companion service for go2rtc: uncomment it to convert any RTSP stream into an instant zero-latency WebRTC stream or MJPEG stream for this page!
